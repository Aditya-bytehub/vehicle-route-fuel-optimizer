// Capabilities

Real algorithmic work, not a map call.

Most "route optimizers" hand your stops to a mapping API and show you what it decided. RouteForge does the math itself — and shows you every step.

Nearest-Neighbor Heuristic

Greedy route construction picks the closest unvisited stop at each step — fast, intuitive, and surprisingly effective for real-world delivery sets.

2-Opt Refinement

After the initial pass, 2-opt swaps crossing edges to untangle the route and shave real distance off every leg — visible, measurable improvement.

Haversine Distance

Great-circle distance between coordinates accounts for Earth's curvature — no flat-map distortions, accurate to within 0.5%.

Fuel Cost Projection

Enter your vehicle's MPG and local fuel price. Total distance becomes dollars — see what each optimization saves you.

Saved Stop Sets

Your stops persist locally. Build a route for today, come back tomorrow, tweak one stop — your set is right where you left it.

Pure Algorithm

No black-box map API doing the thinking for you. The optimization runs in your browser — transparent, inspectable, and instant.
